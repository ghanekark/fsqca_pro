# Charles Ragin - fsQCA Feature Backlog

This document acts as a living product backlog. It contains potential software features extracted directly from Charles Ragin's foundational texts. All proposed features must strictly adhere to the PyQt6 MVC/MVVM architecture defined in `GEMINI.md`.

## 1. UI/UX & Analytical Workflows
*(Features related to views, QThread processing, user interaction, and calibration previews)*
- **Contradiction Resolution Workspace**: Dedicated interface for "Resolving Contradictory Configurations" (p. 48), enabling users to add conditions or refine case selection when identical configurations yield different outcomes.
- **Truth Table Quality Auditor**: Automated diagnostic tool based on "Good Practices (4)" (p. 45) to assess the quality of a truth table, including checks for case distribution, diversity of configurations, and identification of logical remainders. 
- **Venn Diagram Visualization Engine**: Dynamic rendering of Venn diagrams for 3, 4, and 5-condition sets (Figures 3.1-3.5) and adapted multi-value sets (Figure 4.3) to visualize configurational overlap and outcome distribution.
- **Counterfactual Analysis Interface**: Interactive workspace for "Counterfactual Analysis" (p. xiv), allowing users to specify theoretical expectations for "easy" and "difficult" counterfactuals during the minimization process.
- **Small-to-Intermediate N Research Advisor**: Diagnostic tool to evaluate case-to-condition ratios, ensuring the research design stays within the recommended boundaries for "Small-N" and "Intermediate-N" populations (pp. 5-7).
- **Methodological Strategy Wizard**: Onboarding workflow that guides users in selecting the most appropriate CCM technique (csQCA, mvQCA, fsQCA, or MSDO/MDSO) based on data measurement levels and population size (p. 8).
- **Case-to-Configuration Detail Drill-down**: UI bridge between "thick" case-study data and "thin" configurational models, allowing researchers to inspect individual case details from within truth table rows (p. 5).
- **Sequential Technique Pipeline**: Workflow manager for executing csQCA, mvQCA, and fsQCA on the same dataset to validate configurational robustness and added value (Chapter 8).
- **Contextual 'Good Practice' Advisor**: Integrated help system that surfaces 'Good Practice' tips (Box 0.3) relevant to the current analytical step (e.g., case selection, model specification) to ensure methodological rigor.
- **Temporal Path & Critical Juncture Visualizer**: Visualization tools for "Comparative Historical Analysis" that map temporal paths and identify critical junctures where configurations diverge (p. 6).
- **Multi-Scale Research Design Advisor**: Expanded diagnostic tool to evaluate research designs across Small-N, Intermediate-N, and Large-N populations, including recommendations for Micro, Meso, and Macro level scaling (pp. 3-5). [SUPERSEDES: Small-to-Intermediate N Research Advisor]
- **Configurational Exception & Outlier Profiler**: Dedicated interface to analyze cases that deviate from the expected configurations (nonconforming cases), enabling a "dialogue between cases and theory" to refine model parameters (p. 7).
- **Theoretically-Guided Operationalization Wizard**: Integrated interface for defining conditions and outcomes that requires theoretical justification for selected thresholds and intensities, ensuring the model is "theoretically informed" (p. 7).
- **Iterative Case Population Manager**: Supports the "tentative and iterative" nature of case selection, allowing users to add or drop cases with mandatory fields for theoretical justification as new hypotheses arise (p. 23).
- **Case-to-Configuration Ratio & Diversity Monitor**: Real-time diagnostic that calculates the ratio between the "logical space" ($2^k$ for $k$ conditions) and the number of cases, providing visual warnings for the "limited diversity problem" (p. 27). [SUPERSEDES: Multi-Scale Research Design Advisor]
- **Stepwise Condition Reduction Pipeline**: Analytical workflow manager for "stepwise multi-methodological procedures," enabling users to test categories of conditions separately (e.g., via csQCA or Discriminant Analysis) before retaining only high-performing factors for the final model (p. 27).
- **Similarity Level Sensitivity Slider**: Interactive control to toggle between "level 0" (purest) and lower levels (1, 2, etc.) of (dis)similarity, enabling researchers to adjust the stringency of case matching and manage the condition "reservoir" (p. 31).
- **Aggregated (Dis)Similarity Graph Visualizer**: Dynamic rendering of similarity and dissimilarity graphs that visualize case constellations and superimpose different levels of (dis)similarity (p. 31).
- **Focused Qualitative Case Re-interrogation Interface**: Specialized workspace for re-examining MSDO/MDSO-selected case groups (pairwise or in clusters) with "thick" case data, facilitating the "dialogue between theory and data" (p. 31).
- **Informed Dichotomization Wizard**: A tool for converting continuous variables to crisp sets with mandatory justification fields for thresholds, support for mean/median/clustering-based cutoffs, and "direction" validation (ensuring [1] maps to positive theoretical expectation) (Box 3.3, p. 11). [SUPERSEDES: Threshold & Dichotomization Manager]
- **Bidirectional Venn-Table Synchronizer**: An interactive visualization where clicking Venn zones highlights truth table rows and vice versa, supporting the "transformation of tabular data into Venn diagrams and vice versa" (p. 10). [SUPERSEDES: Venn Diagram Visualization Engine]
- **Advanced Truth Table Diagnostic Suite**: Expanded auditor based on "Good Practices (4)" that checks for: (1) outcome balance, (2) counterintuitive configurations (e.g., all [0] leading to [1]), (3) condition proximity/redundancy, and (4) minimum condition variation (the 1/3 rule) (Box 3.5, p. 12). [SUPERSEDES: Truth Table Quality Auditor]
- **Contradiction Resolution Guided Workflow**: Interactive step-by-step wizard implementing the 8 strategies from "Good Practices (6)" (Box 3.6), including adding/removing conditions, threshold adjustment, qualitative re-interrogation, and frequency-based outcome orientation (p. 50-51). [SUPERSEDES: Contradiction Resolution Workspace]
- **Simplifying Assumption Transparency Reporter**: Reporting module that lists all "simplifying assumptions" made during the minimization of logical remainders, enabling users to audit the plausibility of non-observed cases (Box 3.7, p. 64).
- **Multi-Value 'ThresholdSetter' & Visualizer**: Integrated interactive interface for plotting raw data distributions and setting multiple thresholds (n >= 2) inductively (via average linkage clustering) or theoretically, with visual warnings for proximal cases and unbalanced subgroups (p. 80, Box 4.4). [SUPERSEDES: Informed Dichotomization Wizard]
- **mvQCA Complexity & Diversity Monitor**: Real-time calculation of |k| = product(v_i) to assess the explosion of logical space and warn about limited diversity in multi-value models (p. 78). [SUPERSEDES: Case-to-Configuration Ratio & Diversity Monitor]
- **Discrete Fuzzy-Set Template Gallery**: Pre-configured templates for 3-value (0, 0.5, 1), 4-value, and 6-value fuzzy sets to speed up operationalization of data (p. 91, Table 5.1).
- **Two-Step 'Remote-Proximate' Workflow**: [SUPERSEDES: Stepwise Condition Reduction Pipeline]. Specific implementation of Schneider & Wagemann’s (2006) two-step procedure: (1) Minimize "remote" conditions including logical remainders to identify broad causal combinations, and (2) Re-introduce "proximate" conditions for a second-step analysis without remainders to manage limited diversity (pp. 140-141).
- **Condition Selection Strategy Wizard**: [SUPERSEDES: Theoretically-Guided Operationalization Wizard]. Guided interface for selecting conditions using one of the six formal approaches (Comprehensive, Perspective, Significance, Second Look, Conjunctural, Inductive), with prompts for iterative re-operationalization and omitted variable risk assessment (Box 6.1, p. 139).
- **Multi-Step Case Selection & Delineation Wizard**: [SUPERSEDES: Iterative Case Population Manager]. A workflow for defining the "universe" of cases through a 3-step process of geographical/theoretical narrowing, temporal sequencing, and precise operational definition to ensure case-to-context homogeneity (Scouvert 2006, pp. 138-139).
- **Qualitative 'Ideal Type' Dichotomization Assistant**: Interface for dichotomizing conditions by picking "ideal typical" cases (e.g., Switzerland as restrictive, Spain as permissive) and providing historical/qualitative justifications, supporting non-numerical threshold setting (Varone et al. 2006, pp. 142-143).
- **Fuzzy Typology & Ideal-Type Evaluator**: A specialized view for descriptive typology building that measures the distance of cases from theoretical "corners" (ideal types) to evaluate degree of membership rather than causal explanation (Gran 2003, pp. 150-151).
- **Dichotomization Robustness & Sensitivity Dashboard**: [SUPERSEDES: Multi-Value 'ThresholdSetter' & Visualizer]. Interactive interface for executing "systematic sensitivity analyses" by automatically varying dichotomization thresholds and visualizing the resulting shifts in minimal formulas to identify stable "cutoff zones" (p. 151).
- **Deviant/Unique Case Diagnostic View**: [SUPERSEDES: Configurational Exception & Outlier Profiler]. Specialized diagnostic interface that flags configurations representing rare but significant "unsuspected phenomena" (low-frequency paths) to prevent their dismissal as statistical outliers, ensuring all paths are qualitatively "re-interrogated" (pp. 156-157).
- **Qualitative 'Contextual Poverty' Threshold Assistant**: [SUPERSEDES: Qualitative 'Ideal Type' Dichotomization Assistant]. Threshold-setting interface that allows for context-dependent empirical definitions (e.g., "lack of shelter in cold climate" vs "mild climate") with mandatory qualitative justification fields (p. 151).
- **Adaptive QCA Technique Pivot Wizard**: [SUPERSEDES: Sequential Technique Pipeline]. A workflow manager that 
facilitates the transition between csQCA, mvQCA, and fsQCA on the same dataset, allowing researchers to "test different 
techniques" iteratively to find the most meaningful fit for data richness and contradiction resolution (p. 169).
- **Participatory Micro-Case Interaction Log**: An extension of the case drill-down interface designed for micro-level research (individuals), allowing researchers to attach "interaction notes" or "interview transcripts" directly to case data, supporting the "regular interaction" required for participatory research designs (p. 173).
- **Algebraic Hypothesis-to-Result Validator**: A dedicated interface for defining theoretical expectations as Boolean statements (e.g., H1 = A*B*C) prior to analysis. The tool then automatically compares these hypotheses against the complex, parsimonious, and intermediate solutions, highlighting convergences and divergences (p. 174).
- **Distributed Case-Expert Contribution Module**: A feature to export case-specific data packages for external "case specialists" to verify operationalization and provide qualitative context-specific justifications, facilitating "concerted research efforts" across networks of experts (p. 175).
- **Integrated QCA Lexicon & Paradigm Validator**: [SUPERSEDES: Contextual 'Good Practice' Advisor]. An active UI/UX advisor that monitors user-defined labels and report text, flagging non-QCA terminology (e.g., "independent variable", "dependent variable", "regression coefficients") and suggesting correct set-theoretic equivalents ("condition", "outcome", "consistency/coverage") to maintain paradigm integrity (Box G.1).
- **Global QCA Resource Integration Hub**: A dedicated UI component (Help menu/Sidebar) that provides contextual links to external methodological resources, including the COMPASSS textbook page (www.compasss.org/Textbook.htm) and the COMPASSS international database, for real-time access to "didactics" and "working papers" (Appendix: Further Resources).
- **Solution Nesting & Subset Visualizer**: [SUPERSEDES: Parsimony-Complexity Trade-off Visualizer]. A specialized visualization that renders the nested relationship between Complex, Intermediate, and Parsimonious solutions (Parsimonious $\supseteq$ Intermediate $\supseteq$ Complex). It uses Venn-style concentric circles or a tree-structure to show how the intermediate solution incorporates only "easy" counterfactuals, providing a visual audit of the complexity/parsimony continuum (pp. 187-190).
- **Easy Counterfactual 'On-the-Fly' Simplifier**: A UI widget within the truth table view that allows researchers to "test" the removal of a condition from a complex path. It identifies if the resulting remainder is an "easy" counterfactual (consistent with theoretical directionality) and provides immediate feedback on whether the new term remains a subset of the parsimonious solution (pp. 187-188).
- **Truth Table Frequency Filter**: [SUPERSEDES: mvQCA Complexity & Diversity Monitor]. UI control for setting a "frequency threshold" (e.g., N >= 4) that automatically codes rows with insufficient cases (membership > 0.5) as logical remainders ("?"), preventing unreliable outcome assignments based on weak empirical evidence (p. 176, p. 178).
- **Large-N Cumulative Sector Dashboard**: A specialized view for large-N datasets that displays vector space sectors (configurations) sorted by frequency, including a "Cumulative Proportion" column. This allows researchers to identify the concentration of cases (e.g., the top 14 sectors capturing 70% of cases) to determine a robust strength-of-evidence threshold (Table 11.3, p. 177).
- **Cumulative-Capture Frequency Threshold Optimizer**: [SUPERSEDES: Truth Table Frequency Filter]. An algorithmic tool that recommends a frequency threshold by calculating the cumulative proportion of cases captured at various N levels. It targets a specific coverage goal (e.g., capturing 80% of total cases) to filter out low-frequency "noise" likely due to measurement or assignment error (p. 177).
- **Core-Complementary Comparative Table**: [SUPERSEDES: Complexity-Parsimony Continuum Tabular Visualizer]. A specialized results table (Table 11.5) that uses standardized notation (large/small circles) to differentiate between decisive "core" ingredients (parsimonious) and "complementary" ingredients (intermediate) across multiple solution recipes (p. 2).
- **Configurational Sub-Population Sorter**: A UI utility for the results dashboard that allows researchers to group and sort identified recipes according to a specific contextual condition (e.g., "Family Status" in Table 11.6). This facilitates the identification of "sector-specific" recipes and simplifies the interpretation of complex equifinality (pp. 3-4).
- **Asymmetric Dual-Target Calibration Workspace**: [SUPERSEDES: Dual-Target Asymmetric Calibrator]. A dedicated workspace for 'dual-coding' a single continuous variable into two distinct, asymmetric fuzzy sets (e.g., 'High' and 'Low'). It provides side-by-side (or overlaid) S-curve visualizations, allowing researchers to set independent qualitative anchors for each set and verifying that the membership scores are not simply mirror images (negations) of each other, in line with the principle of causal asymmetry (pp. 176-177, 194, 208-209).
- **Momentous Status-Change Dichotomizer**: A diagnostic tool for ordinal variables (like number of children) that prompts researchers to identify "momentous" qualitative transitions (e.g., 0 to 1 child vs. 1 to 2) to justify crisp-set dichotomization. It includes a "Rationale Field" to document why a specific threshold represents a "lifestyle or status constraint" rather than just a quantitative increment (p. 209).

## 2. Data Handling & State Management
*(Features related to models, SQLite persistence, session recovery, and undo/redo stacks)*
- **mvQCA Schema Support**: Implementation of a data model capable of handling "Multi-Value QCA" (Chapter 4) notation and state, allowing for categorical variables beyond binary sets.
- **Threshold & Dichotomization Manager**: Tooling for "Deciding Threshold Values" (p. 76) to facilitate the transition from raw data to crisp or multi-value sets.
- **Legacy QCA Workspace Importer**: Support for QCA-DOS, TOSMANA, and FSQCA file formats to ensure cross-platform research continuity (Box 0.1).
- **Methodological Sample Library**: Pre-loaded 'Inter-war project' (survival/breakdown of democracies) data and configuration files to facilitate learning and benchmarking across csQCA, mvQCA, and fsQCA techniques (Chapter 2).
- **Multi-Level (Macro-Meso-Micro) Project Schema**: Data structures supporting nested levels of analysis (e.g., Coleman’s Bathtub model), allowing for cross-level causal modeling between individuals, organizations, and states (p. 6).
- **Research Transparency & Replicability Logger**: Automated "audit log" that captures all researcher choices—selecting variables, processing them, choosing tools, and intervening during analysis—to ensure the study is "formalized and replicable" (p. 27).
- **'Super-Condition' Logical Aggregator**: Interface for reducing complexity by combining multiple conditions into single conceptual units (e.g., "modernization") through logical operations or data reduction techniques like factor analysis (p. 27).
- **MSDO/MDSO-to-QCA Model Transition Tool**: Workflow bridge that identifies "crucial conditions" characterizing MSDO/MDSO case pairs and automates their selection for model specification in subsequent QCA truth table analysis (p. 31).
- **Boolean Expression Engine & Parser**: Core logic to handle standard csQCA conventions: uppercase for presence [1], lowercase for absence [0], and the dash [-] for "don't care" or missing values, supporting both [*] and space for "AND" operations (Box 3.1).
- **mvQCA Notation & Parser Engine**: Core logic to support bracketed set notation (e.g., CONDITION{0,1,2}) and contraction of values in prime implicants, enabling multi-categorical nominal data support (Box 4.1). [SUPERSEDES: Boolean Expression Engine & Parser]
- **Logical Space 'Reservoir' Policy Manager**: Configuration setting to either "delete" contradictory cases (opening free space for more parsimonious logical remainders) or "keep" them (constraining the reservoir for cautious solutions) (p. 70, note 10). [SUPERSEDES: Manual Configuration Override & Recoding Tool]
- **Fuzzy Operator Logic Engine**: Implementation of Zadeh’s fuzzy algebra: Negation (1 - x), Logical AND (min), and Logical OR (max) for nested configurational expressions (pp. 93-96).
- **Theoretical Directionality Metadata**: Data structures to store researcher expectations for each condition (e.g., "presence should contribute to outcome"), used to filter logical remainders during Intermediate Solution generation (p. 108).
- **Outcome Intersection Logic**: Data model support for defining complex outcome variables as the intersection (minimum score) of multiple simpler outcomes (e.g., High Performance = min(Quality, Productivity)) (Kogut et al. 2004, p. 150).
- **Case-Subunit Temporal Segmenter**: Data management tool for splitting cases into chronologically ordered "subunits" (e.g., Nation_T1, Nation_T2) with metadata tracking for "unit dependence" to alert researchers of reduced independence compared to cross-national cases (p. 159).
- **Logical Remainder Plausibility Filter**: [SUPERSEDES: Simplifying Assumption Transparency Reporter]. Interactive workspace for distinguishing between "plausible" and "non-plausible" logical remainders based on theoretical constraints, ensuring only valid counterfactuals contribute to the parsimonious solution (pp. 152-153).
- **Multi-Level Measurement Schema (Nominal/Ordinal/Interval)**: [SUPERSEDES: mvQCA Schema Support]. Data model enhancement to explicitly tag variables with their measurement levels (Nominal, Ordinal, Interval/Ratio) as defined in the Glossary, enabling automated validation of appropriate QCA techniques (csQCA for Nominal, fsQCA for Interval) and calibration strategies.
- **Causal Recipe State Manager**: Data model for storing "Causal Recipes" as set intersections (min-scores), facilitating the comparison of configurational effects vs. individual condition effects (p. 75).
- **Configurational Density & Exponential Auditor**: [SUPERSEDES: mvQCA Complexity & Diversity Monitor]. A real-time data auditor that monitors the exponential growth of logical space ($2^k$) vs. case count. It provides a visual "density map" to identify logical remainders ("—") and assess the empirical coverage of the configurational space before minimization (p. 13).
- **Outcome Negation State Manager**: Core state support for toggling between the analysis of the presence [1] and absence [0] of the outcome within the truth table dialogue (p. 17). [SUPERSEDES: Negated Outcome Minimization]
- **Metadata-Rich Fuzzy Schema Store**: Data structures to store the "theoretical and substantive knowledge" justifying the translation of ordinal ranks to fuzzy scores, preventing "mechanical" conversion (p. 21). [SUPERSEDES: Calibration Metadata & Audit Log]
- **Fuzzy Compound Set Generator**: [SUPERSEDES: 'Super-Condition' Logical Aggregator]. A data transformation tool that allows users to define new conditions using Zadeh’s fuzzy algebra (Negation: 1-x, Intersection: min, Union: max). It features a real-time "Fuzzy Calculator" that computes the membership scores for the intersection (A·B) or union (A+B) of existing fuzzy sets, supporting the creation of conceptually complex sets like "Affluent countries with low manufacturing employment" (p. 33).
- **Asymmetric Set-Relation State Manager**: State handling for tracking whether the current analytical session is focused on Necessity or Sufficiency, which determines the active consistency formula (ΣX vs ΣY denominator) and the corresponding "violation" logic for XY plots (pp. 120-121).
- **Qualitative Phase Shift Metadata Schema**: [SUPERSEDES: Calibrated Standard Validator]. Data structure for persisting calibration anchors as "qualitative phase shifts" (e.g., the 0°C and 100°C transitions). It mandates the storage of "external, substantive knowledge" alongside numeric thresholds, ensuring that anchors represent theoretical "break-points" rather than just statistical cutoffs (p. 73).
- **Dual-Mode Calibration Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Qualitative Anchor Rationale Logger]. A calibration backend that explicitly toggles between "External/Substantive" calibration (manual anchors based on dependably known standards) and "Inductive/Sample-Specific" calibration (automated anchors based on sample mean/standard deviation). This facilitates the "active" calibration required to move beyond the "passive" and "crude" calibration inherent in conventional statistical indicators (p. 4).
- **Substantive Ratio-Scale Adjustment Log**: [SUPERSEDES: Metadata-Rich Fuzzy Schema Store]. A data structure for recording how precise ratio-scale indicators (e.g., state age) are adjusted based on substantive knowledge (e.g., grouping 500 and 250 years as "early"), preventing "measurement by fiat" (p. 6).
- **Linguistic-to-Fuzzy Mapping Template**: A pre-configured lookup table based on Table 5.1 that maps verbal labels (e.g., "threshold of full membership", "mostly out") to specific membership scores (0.953, 0.181, etc.) and log-odds values (p. 10).
- **Log-Odds & Dual-Scalar Persistence Model**: [SUPERSEDES: Log-Odds Transformation Engine]. Data structure to store the asymmetric scalars ($Scalar_{high}$ and $Scalar_{low}$) and the raw log-odds products (column 4, Table 5.2) alongside final membership scores for auditability and recalibration (Chapter 5, Table 5.2).
- **Multi-Target Variable Calibration Store**: Data model support for persisting multiple, distinct fuzzy-set calibrations (different target sets) for a single raw variable (e.g., "Developed" vs. "Moderately Developed" using the same income data). Each version stores its own qualitative anchors, scalars, and theoretical rationale (pp. 7-8, Tables 5.2 & 5.3).
- **Dual-Method Calibration Persistence Schema**: state management support for storing and versioning fuzzy membership scores generated via both direct (manual anchor) and indirect (regression-based) methods for the same variable, facilitating longitudinal robustness checks (p. 96).
- **Macrovariable 'Logic' Builder**: [SUPERSEDES: Fuzzy Compound Set Generator]. A dedicated "Data Sheet" utility for creating composite conditions (macrovariables) using `fuzzyor` (max) and `fuzzyand` (min) functions, specifically designed to reduce the dimensionality of the vector space by joining substitutable or conjunctive conditions (p. 1, Step 3).
- **Dual-Target Asymmetric Calibrator**: [SUPERSEDES: Multi-Target Variable Calibration Store]. Data management module that facilitates the "dual coding" of a single interval variable into two distinct, asymmetric target sets (e.g., "High AFQT" and "Low AFQT") with independent qualitative anchors, recognizing that non-membership in one does not imply membership in the other (pp. 176-177).
- **Vector Space Sector Occupancy Diagnostic**: [SUPERSEDES: Configurational Density & Exponential Auditor]. A visual diagnostic interface that maps the $2^k$ sectors of the multi-dimensional vector space, specifically highlighting "vacant or mostly vacant sectors" to represent limited diversity in fuzzy-set analysis, moving beyond simple row counts (p. 177).
- **Logical Constraint Validator**: A data-entry and validation tool that allows researchers to define mutually exclusive conditions (e.g., "High Income" and "Low Income"). It automatically calculates the number of "empirically possible" combinations by excluding logically impossible sectors from the $2^k$ vector space (p. 177).
- **Education-to-Fuzzy Discrete Calibration Template**: [SUPERSEDES: Discrete Fuzzy-Set Template Gallery]. A data-driven calibration template for educational attainment that maps specific "years completed" to theoretically-grounded fuzzy scores (e.g., 11 yrs = 0.75, 12 yrs = 1.0, 15 yrs = 0.60 for college), ensuring that "degrees" and "thresholds" are reflected more accurately than linear interpolation (pp. 6-7).
- **Substantive Ratio-Scale Benchmark Library**: [SUPERSEDES: Direct Method Calibration Interface]. A collection of pre-defined qualitative anchors and ratios for common sociological variables (e.g., the 3:2:1 income-to-poverty ratio), allowing researchers to apply "external standards" from foundational literature as calibration presets (p. 6).

## 3. Algorithms & Computational Methodologies
*(Features related to models, minimizers, truth table contradiction resolution, and fuzzy-set math)*
- **MSDO/MDSO Comparison Engine**: A computational module to execute the "MSDO/MDSO: A Systematic Procedure for Matching Cases and Conditions" (p. 28).
- **Negated Outcome Minimization**: Logic to perform "Minimization of the [0] Configurations" (p. 58) to identify the conditions leading to the absence of the outcome.
- **Fuzzy Truth Table Calibration**: Algorithm to map fuzzy sets to "Vector Space Corners" (p. 104) and apply "Frequency Thresholds" (p. 106) for truth table construction.
- **Standardized Minimization Suite**: Implementation of the "Four Complete Minimization Procedures" (p. 64) to generate and compare complex, parsimonious, and intermediate solutions in a single execution cycle.
- **Enhanced MSDO/MDSO Diagnostic Engine**: Implementation of the "Most Similar, Different Outcome" and "Most Different, Same Outcome" logic to systematically match cases and identify causal conditions (p. 8). [SUPERSEDES: Enhanced MSDO/MDSO Diagnostic Engine]
- **Logical Remainder Sensitivity Auditor**: Diagnostic tool to evaluate the impact of different logical remainder treatments on the final solution, addressing common critiques regarding case sensitivity and counterfactuals (Chapter 7).
- **Contradictory Simplifying Assumption Resolver**: Advanced interface and logic for detecting and resolving contradictory simplifying assumptions (Chapter 6) when performing counterfactual analysis in the minimization process. [SUPERSEDES: Counterfactual Analysis Interface]
- **Mill's Canons & MSDO/MDSO Diagnostic Suite**: Integration of Mill's "Method of Agreement", "Method of Difference", and "Joint Method" into the existing MSDO/MDSO framework for more robust causal approximation and hypothesis elimination (pp. 1-2). [SUPERSEDES: Enhanced MSDO/MDSO Diagnostic Engine]
- **Theory-Solution Sorter & Preference Manager**: UI tool for sorting logically equivalent (equally parsimonious) solutions by allowing users to apply theoretical weights or case-based preferences (p. 7).
- **Multidimensional Proximity & Weighting Engine**: computational module for the MSDO/MDSO procedure that calculates "proximity or remoteness" between case pairs in multidimensional space, allowing for user-defined "relative weights" on conditions (p. 28). [SUPERSEDES: Mill's Canons & MSDO/MDSO Diagnostic Suite]
- **Hierarchical Cluster-Based Distance Engine**: Implementation of the 7-step MSDO/MDSO procedure, computing cluster-specific Boolean distance matrices and aggregating them into a comprehensive matrix for cross-case comparison (pp. 30-31). [SUPERSEDES: Multidimensional Proximity & Weighting Engine]
- **Contradiction Detection & "C" Flagging Logic**: Automated identification of configurations where identical combinations of conditions lead to both [0] and [1] outcomes, assigning the "C" label as a prerequisite for iterative resolution (p. 12).
- **Boolean Formula Algebraic Factorization Engine**: Computational module that automatically factors common conditions out of a sum-of-products minimal formula to highlight "core combinations" shared across paths (e.g., GNPCAP * LITERACY * GOVSTAB * { INDLAB + urbaniza }) (p. 57, Box 3.7).
- **Comprehensive Solution Fit Suite**: Implementation of multi-level coverage metrics for minimized solutions, including raw coverage (term-specific), unique coverage (cases uniquely explained), and overall solution coverage (p. 64, Box 3.7).
- **Frequency-Based Outcome Orientation Algorithm**: Probabilistic module for resolving contradictions by assigning the outcome value based on the "most frequently traveled path" within a configuration (Strategy 8, p. 50).
- **Generalized Multi-Value Minimizer**: Implementation of Rule 1: A condition is eliminated if expressions for all n possible values exist and lead to the same outcome (Box 4.2). [SUPERSEDES: Standardized Minimization Suite]
- **Average Distance Clusterer (Inductive Calibration)**: Algorithmic module implementing average linkage cluster analysis to suggest optimal threshold values based on case distributions (p. 80).
- **Pre-Analysis Necessity Filter**: Algorithmic step to identify necessary conditions (Consistency >= 0.9) and optionally exclude them from truth table sufficiency minimization while retaining them in the final solution (p. 107).
- **CSA Intersection & Resolution Auditor**: [SUPERSEDES: Contradictory Simplifying Assumption Resolver]. Automated diagnostic tool that identifies Contradictory Simplifying Assumptions (CSAs) by intersecting [1] and [0] outcome minimal formulas (including logical remainders) and facilitates iterative assignment of fixed outcome values to shared remainders (p. 146).
- **Probabilistic Contradiction Diagnostic (t-test)**: Computational module implementing Roscigno & Hodson's (2004) method for resolving contradictions by using t-tests to compare the outcome distribution within a configuration against the distribution of cases outside that configuration (p. 144).
- **Clustering-based Threshold Distance Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Average Distance Clusterer (Inductive Calibration)]. Algorithmic implementation of Ragin's (1994) clustering technique that assigns cases to truth table rows by calculating Euclidean distance to pre-established cluster corners in n-dimensional standardized space (p. 142).
- **Flexible Contradiction Sensitivity Tester**: Algorithmic module for running parallel minimizations with contradictory rows toggled between [1], [0], and "logical remainder" status to test the robustness and inclusivity of explanatory paths (Nomiya 2001, p. 144).
- **Temporal QCA (TQCA) Trajectory Minimizer**: Computational engine implementing Caren & Panofsky’s (2005) TQCA logic, which treats causal sequences as case attributes and performs Boolean minimization on temporal trajectories while managing the explosion of logical space (pp. 160-161).
- **Threshold Sensitivity Auditor**: Algorithmic module that runs iterative minimizations across a threshold gradient (e.g., 65-75 in increments of 1) to generate a "Stability Index" for identified causal paths (p. 151).
- **Boolean Factorization 'Before & After' Auditor**: [SUPERSEDES: Boolean Formula Algebraic Factorization Engine]. An interactive tool that performs algebraic factorization of minimal formulas and provides a side-by-side comparison of the "narrative" vs. "formal" vs. "factored" notations to ensure transparency and aid interpretation (p. 168).
- **Boolean Distance Calculation Engine**: Algorithmic implementation of "Boolean distance" measurement, calculating the number of dichotomized conditions by which two cases differ, as a fundamental metric for case matching and outlier detection (Glossary).
- **De Morgan’s Law Formula Deducer**: Computational utility that applies De Morgan’s Law to minimized Boolean expressions, allowing researchers to formally derive the logical negation of a causal solution and compare it against empirical results to assess causal asymmetry (p. 57, Index).
- **Sequence Alignment & Gibbs Sampling Engine**: Implementation of Abbott & Barman’s (1997) sequence comparison techniques to identify typical temporal patterns across cases, providing a robust statistical foundation for building configurational trajectories (p. 161, Index).
- **Fuzzy Set Linguistic Qualifier & Operator Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Fuzzy Operator Logic Engine]. Expanded implementation of Zadeh’s fuzzy algebra including standard operators (Negation, min-AND, max-OR) and linguistic hedges: "Concentration" (membership squared for "very") and "Dilation" (square root for "somewhat") to model nuanced theoretical qualifiers (p. 93, Index).
- **Analytic Induction Commonalities Engine**: A computational module that automates the logic of "analytic induction" (Lindesmith 1947) by identifying "causally relevant commonalities" across a user-defined set of cases. It identifies conditions more or less uniformly present in the outcome set, providing a formal algorithmic basis for qualitative induction (Introduction).
- **Asymmetric 2x2 Deconstruction Algorithm**: Logic to partition a 2x2 contingency table into separate consistency scores for necessity and sufficiency, decoupling the causal components fused in a symmetric correlation coefficient (p. 14).
- **Interval/Ratio-to-Fuzzy Calibration Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Fuzzy Truth Table Calibration]. Algorithmic implementation of the two formal methods (Chapter 5) for transforming precise interval and ratio scales into calibrated fuzzy sets based on theoretical benchmarks (p. 74).
- **Set-Theoretic Consistency & Importance Suite**: [SUPERSEDES: Σ-based Consistency & Coverage Calculator]. Computational module for calculating the "consistency and empirical importance" (consistency and coverage) of set-theoretic connections as descriptive tools for refining analysis (Chapter 3, p. 73).
- **Set-Theoretic Decomposition Visualizer**: [SUPERSEDES: Asymmetric Subset Rate Monitor]. Algorithmic diagnostic tool that decomposes the standard 2x2 contingency table (correlational omnibus) into its asymmetric constituents. It explicitly separates the calculation of "necessity" (Cells 1 & 2) from "sufficiency" (Cells 2 & 4) and provides a visual penalty for cases in the "undermining" cells (1 or 4) while neutralizing Cell 3 (absence/absence) to avoid correlational bias (pp. 14-17).
- **Bottom-Up Boolean Simplification Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Generalized Multi-Value Minimizer]. An algorithmic implementation of logical simplification that mirrors manual comparative research: it identifies pairs of configurations where outcomes are consistent but exactly one causal condition varies (presence vs. absence). It visually "collapses" these rows into a simplified configuration, formalizing the core strategy of identifying "key ingredients" for an outcome (p. 13).
- **Fuzzy Subset Violation & Distance Engine**: Computational module that calculates the "degree of violation" for each case relative to the $X=Y$ diagonal. It measures the extent to which $X > Y$ (for sufficiency) or $Y > X$ (for necessity), providing the underlying data for probabilistic consistency measures and identifying "errant cases" that require qualitative re-interrogation (p. 68).
- **Significance-Based Benchmark Validator**: [SUPERSEDES: Probabilistic Benchmark Auditor]. A statistical diagnostic module that implements exact probability tests (at alpha = 0.05) against user-defined benchmarks (e.g., 0.65) to evaluate the significance of consistency scores, providing a rigorous statistical filter for small-N research designs (p. 122).
- **Weighted Set-Theoretic Relevance Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Σ-based Consistency & Coverage Calculator]. Implementation of the refined fuzzy consistency measure that weights cases by their membership strength, ensuring that cases most relevant to the set-theoretic claim (strong members) have a proportional impact on the final consistency score (pp. 124-125).
- **Fuzzy Proportion Penalization Engine** [SUPERSEDES: Significance-Based Benchmark Validator]: Computational module for fuzzy consistency that applies "substantial penalties" for large inconsistencies, ensuring the algorithm is "more compatible with the evidence" than simple crisp proportions (pp. 120-121).
- **Unified Fuzzy-Crisp Probabilistic Engine** [SUPERSEDES: Significance-Based Benchmark Validator]: A computational module that applies the same probabilistic benchmarks (e.g., 0.75) and alpha levels (0.05) to both crisp and fuzzy consistency scores, leveraging the mathematical property that the sum-of-minimums formula returns simple proportions when applied to binary data (p. 121).
- **Context-Aware Consistency & Coverage Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Set-Theoretic Consistency & Importance Suite]. A computational module that calculates fuzzy set-theoretic consistency and coverage using the $\sum[\min(X, Y)]$ intersection. It explicitly handles the "contextual substitution" of denominators: using $\sum(X_i)$ for sufficiency consistency and $\sum(Y_i)$ for sufficiency coverage, while recognizing that the latter is mathematically identical to necessity consistency (Chapter 3).
- **Set Coincidence Diagnostic Engine**: Logic to calculate the degree of coincidence between fuzzy sets ($\Sigma[\min(X_i, Y_i)] / \Sigma[\max(X_i, Y_i)]$), distinguishing coincidence from correlation as a special case of set alignment (p. 120).
- **Fuzzy Coverage Partitioning Engine**: A computational module for equifinal solutions that partitions total coverage into "raw coverage" (total overlap), "unique coverage" (contribution of a path minus other paths), and "overlapping coverage" (intersection of paths) (pp. 121-123).
- **Log-Odds & Dual-Scalar Persistence Model**: [SUPERSEDES: Log-Odds Transformation Engine]. Data structure to store the asymmetric scalars ($Scalar_{high}$ and $Scalar_{low}$) and the raw log-odds products (column 4, Table 5.2) alongside final membership scores for auditability and recalibration (Chapter 5, Table 5.2).
- **Log-Odds Transformation Engine**: A core computational module for the "Direct Method" that translates membership scores to natural log odds ($ln(m / (1-m))$) to ensure symmetry and prevent floor/ceiling effects during calibration (p. 10).
- **Indirect Method Regression Estimator**: A computational engine that rescales raw measures based on a researcher's 6-category qualitative assessment of cases, using a regression estimation procedure to produce fine-grained fuzzy membership scores (p. 9).
- **Dual-Scalar Direct Calibration Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Log-Odds Transformation Engine]. Implementation of the direct method using independent scalars for values above and below the crossover point ($Scalar = 3.0 / |Threshold - Crossover|$), ensuring that the log-odds transformation strictly conforms to external qualitative anchors (Chapter 5, pp. 91-92).
- **Asymptotic Membership Bound Logic**: Computational constraint for the direct method that treats membership scores $\geq 0.95$ as "virtually full membership" and $\leq 0.05$ as "virtually full nonmembership," acknowledging the mathematical impossibility of reaching 1.0 or 0.0 in log-odds space (Chapter 5).
- **Fractional Polynomial Calibration Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Indirect Method Regression Estimator]. Algorithmic implementation using fractional logit models to predict fuzzy membership from qualitative groupings. It supports fractional polynomial expansion of the interval-scale variable to capture non-linear "knowledge-based" rescalings more accurately than linear methods (p. 96).
- **Iterated Nested-Recipe Evaluator**: [SUPERSEDES: Causal Recipe Intersection Evaluator]. A computational engine that automates the comparison of nested causal recipes (e.g., A*B vs A*B*C), calculating changes in consistency and coverage to evaluate the explanatory power of adding/removing specific "essential ingredients" (pp. 11-12).
- **Exhaustive Recipe Subset Auditor**: [SUPERSEDES: Iterated Nested-Recipe Evaluator]. An algorithmic engine that takes a specific causal recipe (e.g., k conditions) and automatically evaluates all $2^k-1$ possible sub-combinations. It generates a summary table of consistency and coverage for each, highlighting the trade-offs as ingredients are removed to identify the optimal balance of parsimony and explanatory power (pp. 320-321).
- **Comparative Probabilistic Consistency/Coverage Engine**: [SUPERSEDES: Significance-Based Benchmark Validator]. A computational module that not only tests scores against benchmarks but also performs statistical significance tests (at user-defined alpha levels) to compare two different consistency or coverage scores for a given N. This allows researchers to rigorously determine if a more complex recipe provides a statistically significant improvement in consistency or coverage over a simpler one (p. 321).
- **Fuzzy-Set Asymmetry Diagnostic Auditor**: A computational diagnostic that calculates consistency scores for both the outcome ($Y$) and its negation ($1-Y$) for each truth table row. It flags "non-complementary" configurations where membership is a subset of both outcomes, empirically validating the "Asymmetry Property" and prompting the researcher to perform independent minimization for both the presence and absence of the outcome (p. 133).
- **Crisp-Fauri Consistency Gap Calculator**: An algorithmic engine that computes the numerical difference in consistency scores between a crisp model (using 0.5 cutoffs) and the underlying fuzzy-set data. It flags configurations that satisfy crisp consistency standards but fail the more stringent fuzzy subset relation ($X \le Y$), preventing the "consistency inflation" inherent in dichotomization (p. 8).
- **Simplified Causal Impediment Minimizer**: A specialized minimization routine that runs in parallel with the main analysis to identify "causal impediments"—conditions whose presence consistently prevents the outcome—providing the algorithmic basis for the Dual-Outcome Asymmetry Reporter (p. 2).
- **Millian Empirical-Pair Matcher**: A computational diagnostic that identifies "ideal comparisons" within the *raw dataset*—pairs of actual cases that differ by exactly one condition. It facilitates the discovery of empirical evidence for a condition's causal significance within a specific configuration, supporting the "profoundly theory dependent" search for matched cases (Mill 1967).
- **INUS Condition & Matched-Case Validator**: A computational tool that identifies if causal conditions in a configuration satisfy the INUS definition. It searches for "matched cases" (actual or counterfactual) that differ by only one condition to demonstrate that the absence of each condition leads to the absence of the outcome (p. 185).
- **Boolean Logit & Probit Regression Engine**: Implementation of Braumoeller's (2003) statistical approach to address limited diversity and "the curse of dimensionality" within a set-theoretic framework (p. 185).
- **Intermediate Solution Redundancy Simplifier**: An algorithmic post-processor for intermediate solutions that identifies and removes terms that have become logically redundant (subsets of other terms in the same solution) after the removal of non-contributing conditions. This ensures the intermediate solution is as succinct as possible while remaining a valid subset of the parsimonious solution (Chapter 9, p. 238).
- **Theory-Driven Term Reduction Assistant**: [SUPERSEDES: Optimal Intermediate Solution Derivation Engine]. A guided computational routine that simplifies complex terms into intermediate ones by inspecting each condition against two criteria: (1) presence in the corresponding parsimonious term, and (2) theoretical "promotion" of the outcome. It automates the removal of "difficult" counterfactuals (those not theoretically supported) while ensuring the resulting term remains a valid subset of the parsimonious result (Chapter 9, p. 237).
- **Continuum Subset Relationship Auditor**: A diagnostic engine that verifies the integrity of the complexity/parsimony continuum by ensuring the strict subset relation ($Complex \subseteq Intermediate \subseteq Parsimonious$) is maintained for every path. It flags any configuration where an intermediate term fails to encompass its complex origin or exceeds the boundaries of its parsimonious destination (Chapter 9, p. 237).
- **Post-Minimization Commonality Diagnostic**: A tool that scans all paths linked to an outcome to identify conditions present in all successful configurations (potential necessary conditions). It alerts the researcher if such a commonality is eliminated in the parsimonious solution, allowing for its manual re-inclusion in the intermediate solution to preserve substantive significance (Chapter 9, p. 237).
- **Additivity & Context-Dependence Diagnostic**: A computational module that identifies "non-additive" causal relationships within identified paths—where a condition's impact is enabled or disabled by the presence of other conditions—and explicitly contrasts these findings with the context-independent "net effects" assumptions of linear regression models (pp. 178-180).
- **Probabilistic Consistency Benchmark Suite**: [SUPERSEDES: Significance-Based Benchmark Validator]. Computational module for testing row consistency against a "benchmark proportion" (e.g., 0.80) using conventional probabilistic criteria (alpha = 0.05), specifically designed to handle "surprising cases" and individual-level non-experimental data (p. 176).
- **Heuristic Consistency Benchmark Advisor**: A diagnostic tool that evaluates set-theoretic consistency scores against the 0.75 "substantial departure" benchmark, providing warnings for causal combinations that fail to meet this heuristic standard of sufficiency (p. 178).
